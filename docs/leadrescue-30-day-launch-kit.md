# LeadRescue AI — 30-Day Launch Kit

## Positioning

**LeadRescue AI (Powered by Axrategy)** helps local service businesses stop losing leads by installing a 7-day lead response and follow-up system.

Core promise: **More booked opportunities from the leads you already receive.**

## What is Being Sold

Not software. Not a chatbot. Not generic AI automation.

You are selling:
- Faster response time
- Fewer missed inquiries
- More booked calls
- Less manual follow-up
- Better customer experience
- A standardized system owners can actually use

## Offer Architecture

### Free Offer
**Lead Leakage Audit**
- 10-point review
- 0–20 scorecard
- Clear diagnosis and recommended package

### Paid Packages

1. **Starter — Lead Capture Fix (CAD $1,500)**
   - Form improvement
   - Instant email response
   - Owner notification
   - Simple tracker
   - Booking link
   - Basic follow-up

2. **Growth — LeadRescue System (CAD $2,500)**
   - Landing section/page
   - Lead form + pipeline
   - Instant response + notifications
   - Booking integration
   - Multi-touch follow-up
   - Review request workflow
   - Training handoff

3. **Premium — Revenue Recovery Engine (CAD $5,000 + monthly support)**
   - Everything in Growth
   - Enhanced landing flow
   - AI-assisted qualification and routing
   - Advanced follow-up + reporting
   - 30-day optimization

## 30-Day Revenue Mix

Target path to CAD $20,000:
- 2 Premium clients × CAD $5,000 = CAD $10,000
- 4 Growth clients × CAD $2,500 = CAD $10,000

Collection model:
- 50% deposit at onboarding
- 50% at go-live
- Optional monthly support (CAD $300–$750)

Day 4: Landing/embed integration
Day 5: End-to-end QA testing
